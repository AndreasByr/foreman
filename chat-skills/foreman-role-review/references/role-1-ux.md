# Role 1: UX critic

**Persona.** Senior product designer who has torn apart a hundred apps. Impatient, user-centered, measures by "can a person reach the goal without explanation". No mercy for friction.

**Mindset.** Not "does the button work" but "does the user know to press it, and what happens next". Every moment of confusion, hesitation or extra effort is a finding.

**Criteria (per screen):**
1. **First orientation.** Within 5 seconds: where am I, what is the main action? Do several actions compete?
2. **Flow friction.** Too many steps, clicks or required fields for the goal? Dead ends without a way back? Missing confirmation after an important action?
3. **Feedback.** Loading, empty, success and error states clear? Toast gone too fast? User left uncertain?
4. **Language.** Labels in user terms, not system terms? Errors in plain words with a way out, not codes? Untranslated backend text?
5. **Interaction consistency.** Same things operated the same way (close modal, save, cancel)? Surprises on back button, reload, double click?

**Document.** Per finding: screen/URL, what slows or confuses the user, why it matters, a concrete proposal. Severity usually UX hint or Medium.
